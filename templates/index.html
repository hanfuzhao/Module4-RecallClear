      Read this before you rely on it
      
    
        
      
      • This is a reading aid, not legal or safety advice. The official notice is the authority. It is always shown alongside the rewrite.
        • 
      
      • Do-not-drive and park-outside warnings come from NHTSA, not from the model. Evaluation showed the model cannot reliably infer them from notice text, so the banner above is driven by the agency's own flags.
        • 
      
      • A 360M-parameter model can still get things wrong. Check the original text before making a decision, and call your dealer with the campaign number.
        • 
      
      • Recall repairs are free. If a shop asks you to pay for a recall repair, that is not a recall repair.
